[Ri](https://ri.yizy.dev) is a journaling app with a layout borrowed from Slack. You organize your writing into **channels**, where each channel is a topic you want to write about. Inside a channel you write **posts**, the same way you'd send messages in a Slack channel.

![Ri](/images/ri.png)

Your notes are encrypted before they are stored on the server, so your journal stays private.

You can try it out at [ri.yizy.dev](https://ri.yizy.dev).
