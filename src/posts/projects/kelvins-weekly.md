I built [Kelvin's Weekly](https://weekly.yizy.dev/) because AI is evolving faster than JS frameworks. It changes day by day, and it seems like new features come out every day. I felt like I was missing out, so I built an aggregator that gathers news from major tech companies and publishes it every week.

## What's in an Edition

A new edition comes out every Sunday, pulling engineering content from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Netflix, Vercel, Cursor and more into one place.

Every entry is structured so you can triage it in seconds:

- **Link** to the original post
- **Why**: a one-sentence filter for whether it's worth your time
- **Key Takeaway**: the core engineering insight, no fluff

Each edition also includes **Kelvin's Pick**, my personal recommendation. It's either a tool I actually use in my workflow or frontier news I find genuinely interesting.

Read the latest edition at [weekly.yizy.dev](https://weekly.yizy.dev/).
